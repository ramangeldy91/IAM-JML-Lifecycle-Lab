# SOP: Employee Offboarding

## 1. Purpose

Define a controlled process for removing or disabling access
when an employee leaves the organization.

## 2. Preconditions

Before making changes, the technician must:

- Verify that the departure request came through an approved channel.
- Confirm the required authorization and effective date/time.
- Identify the employee's accounts, assigned roles, and relevant systems.
- Follow the organization's offboarding and evidence-retention policies.

## 3. Procedure

### Step 1 — Validate the Request

Confirm the employee identity, departure date/time,
and required approvals.

If the request is incomplete or cannot be verified,
escalate it through the approved process.

### Step 2 — Disable Sign-In

At the authorized effective time, disable or block sign-in
using the approved identity-management system.

For urgent security-related departures, follow the organization's
incident and escalation procedures.

### Step 3 — Revoke Active Access

Revoke active sessions, refresh tokens, or other credentials
where supported and required by policy.

Disabling an account does not necessarily terminate every
existing session immediately in every system.

### Step 4 — Review Connected Systems

Check relevant applications, groups, roles, remote access,
and other entitlements according to the offboarding checklist.

Remove or disable access in accordance with policy.
Consider hybrid identity dependencies and the authoritative
identity source where applicable.

### Step 5 — Recover Company Assets

Coordinate device, badge, and other asset recovery
with the responsible team.

Record outstanding items and escalate unresolved issues.

### Step 6 — Verify the Final State

Verify that:

- The account is disabled or blocked as required.
- Relevant access and entitlements have been addressed.
- Session revocation actions have been completed where applicable.
- Outstanding assets or exceptions are documented.

### Step 7 — Document and Close

Record the actions taken, timestamps, verification results,
exceptions, and required approvals.

Retain records according to the applicable policy.
Do not delete the account or its records unless authorized.

## 4. Exception Handling

If access cannot be disabled, a session remains active,
or an entitlement cannot be removed, escalate promptly
and document the remaining risk and follow-up actions.

## 5. Lab Status

This is a proposed procedure for a simulated training lab.
Technical implementation and test evidence will be documented separately.
